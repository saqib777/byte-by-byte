# Daily Dive - One Concept a Day

> A daily learning log covering Computer Science, Algorithms, AI/ML, Robotics, Electronics, Nature, and Technology — one focused concept at a time, understood deeply enough to explain, implement, and apply.

## Why this repo exists

Most "100 days of code" repos collect syntax exercises that get forgotten. This one is different: each entry is a single concept, chosen deliberately, with the goal of actually *understanding* it — not just seeing it once. The scope is intentionally broad (CS, algorithms, nature, electronics, AI/ML, robotics) because the best ideas often transfer across domains.

## How it works

Each day gets one topic and one entry, following a consistent format:

- **What it is** - a plain-language description of the concept
- **Why this topic** - the reasoning behind picking it, and its relevance now and going forward
- **How to learn it** - a step-by-step path (resources, order, what to skip on a first pass)
- **Time estimate** - realistic time to reach solid understanding
- **Applications** - how it connects to career (SDET / QA Automation), further learning, or everyday life
- **Implementation** *(where applicable)* — working code, tests, or a small demo
